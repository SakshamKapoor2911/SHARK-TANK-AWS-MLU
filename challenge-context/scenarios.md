# Shark Tank Challenge: Detailed Scenarios Breakdown

This document provides a deep dive into each of the 5 scenarios from the **Cloud Connect — Howard 2026 Student Scenario Pack**, outlining student pain points, technical requirements, stretch opportunities, and strategic pitch angles.

---

## Scenario 1: Fuel Up: Healthy Eating Coach
- **Domain**: Wellness • Nutrition
- **Target Persona**: First-year students living on instant ramen, late-night dining hall runs, and vending machine snacks with minimal kitchen equipment and tight budgets.

### The Mission
Help students eat better on a shoestring budget and demanding academic schedule without feeling overwhelmed or shamed.

### Required Features ("Must Let a Student"):
1. **Pantry / Dining Hall Recommender**: Enter current fridge, pantry, or dining-hall options and instantly get quick, healthy, achievable meal ideas.
2. **Budget-Bounded Grocery Planner**: Generate an itemized grocery list calibrated to a user-specified weekly budget (e.g., $30, $50, $75).
3. **Judgement-Free Nutrition Q&A**: Ask any dietary or nutrition questions and receive approachable, friendly, non-judgmental guidance.

### Stretch Goals (Bonus Points):
- 🎨 **Motivational 'Meal of the Day' Image**: AI-generated visual showcase of a healthy, delicious meal.
- 🔄 **Smart Goal-Based Swaps**: Suggest healthy ingredient or meal swaps aligned with student goals (e.g., "swap energy drink for matcha/protein snack for sustained focus").
- 🥗 **Dietary & Cultural Inclusivity**: Adapt recommendations dynamically for allergies, vegan/vegetarian, halal, kosher, or gluten-free diets.

### Pitch Hook Ideas:
> *"What if your dining hall swipe didn't mean a food coma or a salt overload? 'Fuel Up' turns leftover dining hall sides and dorm microwave staples into balanced power meals for under $4 a day."*

## Scenario 2: Study Buddy: The AI Learning Sidekick
- **Domain**: Academics • Productivity
- **Target Persona**: Undergrads tackling intimidating STEM, humanities, or business courses who struggle with solitary studying, procrastination, and pre-exam cramming panic.

### The Mission
Studying alone is isolating and tedious. Build an AI companion that demystifies complex coursework and keeps students accountable and on track for midterms and finals.

### Required Features ("Must Let a Student"):
1. **Plain-English Explainer**: Paste in lecture slides, messy notes, or complex academic concepts and receive clear, jargon-free explanations.
2. **Active Recall Engine**: Automatically generate relevant practice quiz questions, flashcards, or interactive retrieval practice tests.
3. **Cram & Exam Countdown Planner**: Generate a structured, realistic day-by-day study timetable leading up to exam day.

### Stretch Goals (Bonus Points):
- 🎭 **Customizable Tutor Persona**: Let the student toggle tutor personalities (e.g., "Supportive Coach", "Socratic Professor", "Gen-Z Peer Explainer", "Gordon Ramsay of Coding").
- 🎚️ **3-Tiered Concept Breakdown**: Explain any topic at three graduated depths (e.g., ELI5 / High School / College Senior).
- 🖼️ **Motivational Study-Session Visual**: Generate inspiring, aesthetic study-session images (e.g., lofi study cafe, triumph after exams).

### Pitch Hook Ideas:
> *"Office hours are only 2 hours a week, but Study Buddy is in your pocket 24/7. Turn a 40-page confusing lecture PDF into digestible flashcards and an 8-day stress-free exam roadmap."*

## Scenario 3: Money Moves: Student Budget Planner
- **Domain**: Finance • Life Skills
- **Target Persona**: College students experiencing independent financial management for the first time, juggling financial aid refunds, textbook costs, subscriptions, and weekend spending.

### The Mission
Financial management in college is intimidating and rarely taught in school. Build an accessible app that demystifies cash flow and empowers students to make proactive financial decisions.

### Required Features ("Must Let a Student"):
1. **Cash Flow Visualizer / Tracker**: Input income (jobs, allowances, stipends) and expenses (rent, dining, Uber, entertainment) to see exactly where money goes.
2. **Adaptive Monthly Budget**: Deliver a clean, flexible monthly budget breakdown that students can tweak with real-time feedback.
3. **Plain-English Financial Advisor**: Ask tricky money questions (credit scores, loans, saving tactics) and receive straightforward, actionable answers.

### Stretch Goals (Bonus Points):
- ✂️ **Spending Cut Recommender**: Analyze spending habits and identify painless, realistic areas to cut costs.
- 🎯 **Targeted Savings Goal Calculator**: Set a specific milestone (e.g., spring break trip, emergency fund, new laptop) and project the timeline based on weekly contributions.
- 🏆 **'Financial Wins' Celebrator**: Generate encouraging, celebratory summaries of smart financial choices to reinforce positive habits.

### Pitch Hook Ideas:
> *"Most students don't need a Wall Street spreadsheet; they need to know if they can afford pizza tonight without overdrafting next week. Money Moves gamifies financial literacy for dorm life."*

## Scenario 4: Campus Compass: Navigate College Life
- **Domain**: Community • Belonging
- **Target Persona**: Freshmen, transfer students, and first-generation college students feeling overwhelmed by a new campus culture, sprawling facilities, and social barriers.

### The Mission
New students often feel lost—unclear on where to go, what's happening around campus, or whom to contact for help. Build a friendly, welcoming navigator that accelerates campus belonging.

### Required Features ("Must Let a Student"):
1. **Campus Life Conversational Concierge**: Ask practical questions about campus rules, traditions, facilities, or culture and receive friendly, insider answers.
2. **Interest-Matched Extracurricular Guide**: Recommend student organizations, affinity groups, and upcoming campus events based on personal hobbies and academic majors.
3. **Direct Problem-to-Resource Router**: Input an issue (e.g., "lost my ID card", "struggling with roommate", "need emergency tutoring") and immediately get routed to the exact office, building, and contact.

### Stretch Goals (Bonus Points):
- 📋 **Personalized 'First-Week Checklist'**: Curate an interactive onboarding to-do list tailored to a student's year, housing, and background.
- 🤝 **Friendship & Connection Catalyst**: Suggest organic, low-pressure campus activities and conversation prompts to build lasting friendships.
- 🎨 **Club/Event Hype Poster Generator**: Create vibrant promotional imagery and copy for student clubs, tailgates, or hackathons.

### Pitch Hook Ideas:
> *"Every university has 300 clubs and 50 admin buildings, but zero intuitive manuals. Campus Compass is your senior-year mentor in your pocket from day one."*

## Scenario 5: Balance: Wellness & Stress Companion
- **Domain**: Mental Health • Student Wellbeing
- **Target Persona**: Students experiencing burnout, academic anxiety, homesickness, or social pressure who need low-friction, confidential emotional support.

### The Mission
College life comes with intense academic deadlines, social shifts, and performance anxiety. Build an empathetic companion that helps students pause, decompress, and build sustainable wellness habits.

### Inferred Core Capabilities:
1. **Mood & Stress Check-in**: Quick daily sentiment check-ins providing empathetic feedback and personalized coping mechanisms.
2. **Micro-Reset Routines**: Step-by-step guided breathing, box-breathing animations, desk stretches, or mindfulness breaks between study blocks.
3. **Campus Resource Referral**: Safe, clear, non-stigmatizing pathways to university counseling centers, wellness spaces, and crisis helplines.

### Potential Stretch Goals:
- 🧘 **Personalized Self-Care Challenges**: Weekly bite-sized habit builders (hydration, sleep hygiene, digital detox).
- 📈 **Stress Triggers & Trend Analysis**: Spot patterns (e.g., "Stress peaks every Wednesday before Chem lab").
- ☀️ **Affirmation & Calming Visualizer**: AI-generated calming art and contextual affirmations tailored to specific anxieties.

### Pitch Hook Ideas:
> *"You can't pour from an empty cup. Balance ensures Howard students maintain mental and emotional clarity during midterms, finals, and everything in between."*
