## 2. What the Sharks Look For (Judging Criteria Breakdown)

The official evaluation pillars and how Prismatic scores 100%:

### 1. Creativity — *"Fresh and clever?"*
* **The Prism Metaphor**: Most teams will present a standard chatbot. Prismatic frames learning as **cognitive refraction** (white light splitting into multi-modal spectrums), a memorable and differentiated concept.

### 2. Usefulness — *"Solves a real student problem?"*
* **Root Pain Point**: Directly addresses 2026 undergraduate **task paralysis, dining hall study sessions, and pre-exam anxiety**. Students don't want 20 pages of text; they want high-yield retention.

### 3. Execution & Concept — *"Is the idea well built out?"*
* **Full Multi-Widget Pipeline**: Built on Amazon Quick with 6 interconnected widgets (User Input $\rightarrow$ 5-Question Form $\rightarrow$ Analogy Generator $\rightarrow$ ASCII Schema Mapper $\rightarrow$ Socratic Chat $\rightarrow$ Visual Memory Anchor).

### 4. Pitch & Teamwork — *"Clear, confident, everyone contributes?"*
* **Every Teammate Has a Defined Role**: Every single member of the ~10 person team has an active, visible job during the 2 minutes.

## 4. How Your Room Picks a Champion (Voting Strategy)

1. **One Vote Per Team**: Each team gets 1 vote, cast by your designated representative (Member #9).
2. **No Self-Voting**: You cannot vote for yourself.
3. **Top Votes Wins**: The team with the most votes is crowned **Room Champion** and moves to the **Main-Stage Final**.

### 💡 The Room Champion Winning Strategy:
- **Be the Most Energetic**: Room pitches happen after a long sprint. Start with a loud, confident, smiling introduction.
- **Relatable Dorm Examples**: When explaining Spectrum 1, use the dining hall rush or laundry queue analogy—fellow students will laugh, relate, and vote for you.
- **Flawless Timing**: Finishing at **1 minute 55 seconds** proves discipline and professionalism.
- **End with the Exact Formula**: The handout specifically looks for: *"With more time we'd add live Canvas integration and real-time audio study groups."* Using the official phrasing proves you paid attention to the criteria!
