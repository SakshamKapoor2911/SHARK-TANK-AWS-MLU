## 🛡️ Judge Q&A Defense Sheet (Anticipated Shark Questions)

### Q1: *"How does this differ from just typing 'explain this to me' into ChatGPT?"*
> **Answer**: *"Great question! ChatGPT gives one generic wall of text that students glaze over. Prismatic is an agentic pipeline: it first diagnoses the student's cognitive friction points through a 5-question triage, and then orchestrates 4 distinct, simultaneous modalities—including Socratic sparring where the AI refuses to just give away answers and instead forces active retrieval."*

### Q2: *"Why did you choose Amazon Quick instead of writing custom code?"*
> **Answer**: *"Speed and multi-widget pipeline orchestration. In Amazon Quick's Apps mode, we chained user intake directly into multiple specialized Amazon Bedrock foundation models—text synthesis, image generation, and a stateful Socratic chatbot—all running cohesively on one screen in under 75 minutes."*

### Q3: *"How do you keep students from using this just to cheat?"*
> **Answer**: *"Prismatic is intentionally designed for **learning retention, not answer-copying**. The Socratic Sparring module strictly limits responses to 2 sentences and asks conceptual 'why' questions. It won't write your paper; it trains you to master the underlying mechanics so you ace the exam."*
