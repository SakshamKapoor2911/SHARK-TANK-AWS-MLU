## 4. Shark Q&A Defense Master Cheat Sheet

1. **"Can't students just prompt ChatGPT with their slides?"**  
   *Defense*: *"ChatGPT generates passive 800-word text walls that cause cognitive overload. Prismatic is an agentic pipeline: it triages the student's cognitive intake in 30 seconds and outputs 4 simultaneous modalities—including a Socratic partner that refuses to lecture and only quizzes edge cases."*
2. **"Why Amazon Quick instead of custom code on AWS Amplify?"**  
   *Defense*: *"Speed to value and zero infrastructure risk. Amazon Quick gives us native multi-widget event streaming, zero-infra scaling, and direct Bedrock integration in 60 minutes with zero CVE vulnerabilities. University IT can deploy it via AWS IAM in minutes without auditing custom backends."*
3. **"How do you prevent Socratic AI from hallucinating before an exam?"**  
   *Defense*: *"Dual-layer guardrails: First, Bedrock foundation models are prompted with strict zero-shot syllabus grounding. Second, our Socratic engine is hard-capped to 2 sentences and forbidden from making assertions—it only asks probing counter-questions to make the student verify the syllabus invariant themselves."*
4. **"What is the revenue model?"**  
   *Defense*: *"B2B enterprise university licensing via Canvas LMS integration, plus a freemium B2C tier. Universities spend millions annually on student retention; Prismatic directly improves DFW (Drop/Fail/Withdraw) rates."*
5. **"What if symposium Wi-Fi crashes during your pitch?"**  
   *Defense*: *"We never pause. Our Display Pilot instantly pivots the laptop showing the local offline Prismatic Web App, which runs zero-dependency HTML5 with pre-rendered SVG schemas, TTS audio, and deterministic Socratic chat without a single network packet."*
